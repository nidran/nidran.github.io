Regularization

Say it in 30s: Anything that constrains a model to reduce variance at the cost of a little bias. Most often an added penalty on model complexity: minimize (empirical loss + λ·penalty). λ trades fit against simplicity.

L1 vs L2 (the classic question)

  • L2 / Ridge (penalize Σwᵢ²): shrinks weights smoothly toward zero, never exactly zero. Handles correlated features by sharing weight. Has a closed form.
  • L1 / Lasso (penalize Σ|wᵢ|): drives some weights exactly to zero → built-in feature selection / sparsity. The corner of the diamond constraint is why. No closed form (subgradient / soft-thresholding at zero).
  • Elastic Net: L1 + L2, gets sparsity plus stability with correlated features.

Beyond penalties: early stopping, dropout, data augmentation, weight decay, ensembling are all variance-fighting regularizers.

Trap: larger λ = more regularization = more bias, less variance. Tune λ on validation, not test.

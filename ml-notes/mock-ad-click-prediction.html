Mock - Ad Click Prediction

Prompt: Design a system that predicts whether a user will click on a given ad, powering the ad auction and ranking for a large social media platform. ~2B DAU, ~10M active ad campaigns, billions of impression decisions daily.

See the spine: ML System Design Framework.

#What I covered

  • Objective — eventually got to advertiser revenue, but took multiple prompts. Did not arrive at bid × pCTR × pCVR or eCPM on my own.
  • Retrieval — correctly proposed two-tower with ANN lookup; understood decoupled serving (precompute ad embeddings offline, compute user embedding at inference).
  • Ranking — identified that a richer model is needed post-retrieval but could not name the architecture (DCN, Wide & Deep, DeepFM). Went blank.
  • Re-ranking — mentioned diversity and business rules; mentioned position bias unprompted.
  • Training data — described features instead of labels when asked. Did not surface downsampling/recalibration, delayed conversions, or selection bias (ESMM).
  • Serving — could not describe the inference flow, feature store tiers, or latency budget breakdown.
  • Monitoring — mentioned drift but initially confused predicted CTR dropping with model degradation. Did not mention calibration ratio or NE.
  • Guardrails — did not volunteer click fraud, legal targeting constraints, or budget pacing.

#Key concepts taught during mock

#Business objective

  • Revenue = Σ (bid × pCTR × pCVR) for CPA bids (CPC: bid × pCTR; CPM: bid); ranking by eCPM per bid type
  • GSP/VCG-style auction (second-price in the single-slot case); normalize CPC/CPM/CPA into common currency
  • Calibration matters because probabilities directly feed auction pricing

#Ranking model (pCTR / pCVR)

  • Industry standard: Deep & Cross Network (DCN) or Wide & Deep
  • Why: explicit feature crosses are king in CTR prediction (user segment × ad category × context)
  • Evolution: logistic regression → GBDT → Wide & Deep → DeepFM → DCN
  • Key difference from retrieval: can now use cross-features (user-ad interaction features)

#Position bias

  • Ads higher in feed get more clicks regardless of relevance
  • Fix: train with position as feature, drop at inference
  • Shallow tower (Google/YouTube) or PAL (Huawei)

#Training data gotchas

  • Labels: click=1/no-click=0 for pCTR; conversion=1/no-conversion=0 for pCVR
  • Class imbalance: CTR ~1-3%. Use negative downsampling + recalibrate: p_true = p_sampled / (p_sampled + (1-p_sampled)/sampling_rate)
  • Delayed conversions: tension between freshness and completeness of conversion labels
  • Selection bias in pCVR: only observe conversions on clicked ads; ESMM (Alibaba) trains pCVR on all impressions

#Serving architecture

  • Offline features: user profiles, historical aggregates, ad embeddings → feature store (Redis/KV), sub-ms lookup
  • Near-real-time features: streaming via Kafka + Flink, e.g., "user impressions last hour" → seconds-stale
  • Real-time features: computed at request time (position, time, context)
  • Flow: request → retrieval (ANN, <10ms) → feature assembly (batch KV lookup, 5-10ms) → ranking inference (10-20ms) → auction + business rules (5ms) → total ~50ms
  • Prediction service (TF Serving / Triton) separate from feature store

#Ads-specific concerns (should volunteer)

  • Click fraud: bots/competitors draining budgets; need fraud detection layer
  • Feedback loops: model predictions affect training data distribution
  • Fairness/legal: housing, employment, credit ads cannot target by protected attributes
  • Budget pacing: smooth advertiser spend across the day, don't exhaust budget early

#Monitoring

  • Calibration ratio (predicted/observed CTR) sliced by advertiser, category, segment
  • Normalized cross-entropy (NE) — better than AUC because it measures calibration, not just ranking
  • Revenue metrics: RPM, eCPM
  • Auction health: unexpected shifts in winning bids

#Gaps to close before next mock

  1. DCN / Wide & Deep architecture — read the Google papers, know the diagram
  2. Feature serving tiers and latency budgets
  3. Training data: downsampling + calibration formula, delayed conversions, ESMM
  4. Proactively volunteer: click fraud, pacing, legal constraints, monitoring
  5. Commit to specifics faster — don't circle on business objective

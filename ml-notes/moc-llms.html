LLMs

The transformer architecture, how LLMs are trained, adapted and grounded (SFT, RLHF/DPO, LoRA, RAG), and how to evaluate LLM and RAG systems.

#If asked to explain a transformer

Walk the shapes: tokens to embeddings, Q,K,VQ,K,VQ,K,V, softmax(QK⊤/dk)V\text{softmax}(QK^\top/\sqrt{d_k})Vsoftmax(QK⊤/dk​​)V with an n×nn\times nn×n score matrix, then multi-head, positional information, causal mask, residual+norm, FFN. Close with the O(n2)O(n^2)O(n2) cost and how the KV cache, GQA and FlashAttention address it.

#If asked "RAG, fine-tuning, or prompting?"

Diagnose the failure first: missing or changing knowledge points to RAG, behavior or format to fine-tuning, and everything starts with prompting plus an eval set.

#If asked "how do you eval an LLM"

Split into reference-based (BLEU/ROUGE/exact match), reference-free (LLM-as-judge), RAG-specific (faithfulness, context relevance), and human evals. Then raise the meta-question: how do you trust the judge?

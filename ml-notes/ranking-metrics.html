Ranking Metrics: NDCG, MAP & MRR

Say it in 30s: Search and recommenders care about order, not a single label. MRR rewards the rank of the first relevant hit. MAP averages precision over all relevant items (binary relevance). NDCG handles graded relevance with position discount: DCG@k=∑i=1k2reli−1log⁡2(i+1)\mathrm{DCG@k} = \sum_{i=1}^{k} \frac{2^{rel_i}-1}{\log_2(i+1)}DCG@k=∑i=1k​log2​(i+1)2reli​−1​, then NDCG@k=DCG@k/IDCG@k\mathrm{NDCG@k} = \mathrm{DCG@k}/\mathrm{IDCG@k}NDCG@k=DCG@k/IDCG@k. Pick the metric to match the feedback type and how many “right answers” each query has.

Key points

  • Position discount: relevance at rank 1 counts more than at rank 10 — all three metrics bake that in.
  • MRR = mean of 1/rank of first relevant1/\text{rank of first relevant}1/rank of first relevant — one gold answer (entity lookup, “I’m feeling lucky”).
  • MAP = mean of average precision per query — several relevant docs, yes/no labels.
  • NDCG normalizes against the ideal ranking so scores are comparable across queries.

Common follow-ups / traps

  • "Why not accuracy on the top item?" → Ignores the list structure and graded quality of lower ranks.
  • Offline NDCG is a proxy — confirm lifts with online interleaving or A/B tests on engagement (A/B Testing).

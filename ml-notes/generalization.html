Generalization

Say it in 30s: A model generalizes if it performs well on data it never saw during training. The whole point of ML is low error on the true distribution, not low error on the training set. Held-out data is how we estimate it.

Key points

  • The i.i.d. assumption is the license to even hope for generalization: test data is statistically like training data.
  • Train / validation / test split: train fits parameters, validation tunes hyperparameters and model choice, test is touched once for an unbiased final estimate.
  • Distribution shift is the #1 reason good offline models disappoint in production — the i.i.d. assumption quietly fails.
  • The generalization gap = test error − training error; large gap signals overfitting.

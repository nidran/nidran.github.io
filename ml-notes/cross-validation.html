Cross-Validation

Say it in 30s: A way to estimate generalization without wasting data on a fixed validation set. k-fold CV splits the data into k parts, trains on k−1 and validates on the held-out fold, rotates k times, and averages. You get a more stable performance estimate and a sense of its variance.

Variants

  • k-fold (k=5 or 10 typical): the default.
  • Stratified k-fold: preserve class ratios per fold — use for imbalanced classification.
  • Leave-one-out: k = n, low bias, high variance, expensive.
  • Group k-fold: keep correlated rows (same user/patient) together to avoid leakage.
  • Time-series split: train on past, validate on future — never shuffle time. Random k-fold leaks the future.

Traps

  • Do all preprocessing (scaling, feature selection) inside the fold, or you leak.
  • CV tunes hyperparameters; still keep a final untouched test set.

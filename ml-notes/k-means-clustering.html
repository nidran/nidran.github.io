k-Means Clustering

Say it in 30s: k-means partitions nnn points into kkk clusters by alternating assignment to nearest centroids and updating centroids to cluster means (Lloyd's algorithm). It minimizes within-cluster sum of squared distances — a non-convex objective, so initialization matters; k-means++ spreads initial centers and is the default fix. It assumes roughly spherical, equal-variance clusters and needs feature scaling; pick kkk with elbow/silhouette and validate on the business question, not the plot alone.

Key points

  • Objective: min⁡{Cj}∑j=1k∑x∈Cj∥x−μj∥22\displaystyle \min_{\{C_j\}} \sum_{j=1}^{k} \sum_{x \in C_j} \Vert x - \mu_j \Vert_2^2{Cj​}min​j=1∑k​x∈Cj​∑​∥x−μj​∥22​.
  • Hard assignment only; for soft clusters use a GMM + EM (k-means ≈ GMM with equal spherical covariances in the limit).
  • Failure modes: wrong kkk, non-convex blobs, varying cluster sizes/densities, outliers pulling centroids — DBSCAN/HDBSCAN or GMM may fit better.
  • Always scale continuous features; Euclidean distance is scale-sensitive.

Common follow-ups / traps

  • "How choose kkk?" → elbow on inertia (with caveats), silhouette (cohesion vs separation), domain knowledge; with labels, ARI/NMI on held-out structure.
  • "Guaranteed optimum?" → No — Lloyd's only finds local minima; run multiple restarts.
  • "k-means on categoricals?" → not without encoding; consider k-modes or model-based clustering.

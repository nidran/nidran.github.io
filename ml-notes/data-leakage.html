Data Leakage

Say it in 30s: Leakage is any path for test or future information to influence training or model selection, so offline metrics lie and production tanks. Taxonomy: target leakage (features that encode the label or its future), train–test contamination (duplicate users/docs across splits), temporal leakage (training on the future), and preprocessing leakage (scalers, encoders, or feature selection fit on the full dataset). Fix by splitting first, then fitting every transform inside the training fold only.

Key points

  • Target leakage example: “account closed date” predicting churn — only known after the user left.
  • Group splits when rows correlate (patient, user, session); random shuffles let the model memorize entities.
  • Time series: always train on past, validate on future — random k-fold is invalid.

The math

Preprocessing leakage: fit scaler on train only. Target encoding: naive category mean μ^cat=1ncat∑i∈catyi\hat\mu_{\mathrm{cat}} = \frac{1}{n_{\mathrm{cat}}}\sum_{i \in \mathrm{cat}} y_iμ^​cat​=ncat​1​∑i∈cat​yi​ uses the row’s own label in the average when that row is included → leakage. Leave-one-out / out-of-fold fix with global mean μ\muμ and smoothing mmm:

e^cat=ncat yˉcat+m μncat+m\hat e_{\mathrm{cat}} = \frac{n_{\mathrm{cat}}\, \bar y_{\mathrm{cat}} + m\, \mu}{n_{\mathrm{cat}} + m}e^cat​=ncat​+mncat​yˉ​cat​+mμ​

Compute yˉcat\bar y_{\mathrm{cat}}yˉ​cat​ only on other rows in the training fold (or LOO neighbors); mmm shrinks small categories toward μ\muμ.

Common follow-ups / traps

  • "We scaled before splitting — is that bad?" → Yes — test statistics leaked into train normalization.
  • "CV score is 99% — suspicious?" → Walk the leakage checklist before celebrating.
